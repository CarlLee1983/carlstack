## Final prompts

### ossscanner-vulnerability-report-triage

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original editorial technical article cover, horizontal 16:9, no lettering. Main idea: automated vulnerability reports need human triage and verification. Render a bold handmade charcoal and dry-brush editorial print on warm ivory paper, with selective vermilion accents. Close oblique view of a human hand holding a magnifying lens over one sheet taken from an untidy fan of report papers; under the lens a small ambiguous broken-line mark becomes sharply inspectable, while the many other sheets have hazy nonlinguistic marks. This is the act of examining evidence, no approval tick and no claim all reports are true. Tactile charcoal grain, torn-paper edges, expressive limited marks, sophisticated magazine illustration. Large hand and circular lens form the focal point at the middle; broad diagonal paper stack, plenty of negative space, strong legibility at 320px crop. Matte flat ink, no photographic desk objects. No text, digits, letters, logos, dashboard, UI, robot, AI brain, shields, padlock, code screenshot, tiny annotations or generic neon cyber imagery.

### splunk-mcp-destination-credential-boundary

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original editorial technical article cover, horizontal 16:9. Main idea: a credential is valid only for its specifically matched destination, not every endpoint a tool could address. Build a surreal architectural still life from matte pale mint plaster and terracotta clay. Two separate freestanding architectural walls on a coral-sand ground, one nearby wall with a distinctive three-lobed aperture and a further wall with an incompatible rectangular aperture. A single chunky mint three-lobed credential piece floats immediately in front of its matching aperture, alignment unmistakable; nothing connects to the incompatible portal. Sculptural object, not an actual key or security lock. Side-on low architectural perspective, crisp long afternoon shadows, burnt coral and pale seafoam palette, analog medium-format product photo aesthetic, quiet precise boundaries. Central matched aperture and credential survive centered 16:9 thumbnail crop; wide asymmetrical composition; no text, numbers, logos, UI, diagrams, arrows, screens, dashboards, glass tubes, conveyor tracks, safes, circuitry, glowing AI brain.

### claude-science-uvmap-provenance

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original editorial technical article cover, horizontal 16:9. Main idea: scientific provenance must distinguish observed measurements from transformations and uncertain reconstruction. Create an unmistakably handmade cyanotype and translucent drafting-vellum collage, NOT a real scientific map. A dark indigo handmade-paper sheet with a sparse grouping of coarse white punched dots lies beneath one lifted translucent warm-peach tracing sheet bearing broad sweeping broken contour strokes, some confidently opaque and others fading into incomplete wisps. The layers are physically separated by a lifted folded corner and cast shadows: underlying coarse dots are observations; top speculative handpainted arcs are interpretation with uncertainty. Composition is a large off-center ellipse made from irregular torn papers viewed overhead, cropped boldly against deep midnight blue paper. Tactile paper fibers, indigo photographic print grain, soft diffuse light and gentle peach highlights. Main subject fits central 80 percent for small card crop. Pure conceptual art: no accurate celestial coordinates, actual constellations, real data, scientific legend, scale, text, letters, numerals, logos, photographic galaxy, dashboard, UI, AI brain, lens, hand, portals, geometric machine parts.
